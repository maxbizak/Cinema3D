# Third-party tools

The installer downloads these tools directly from their publishers; this repository and its release ZIP do not redistribute their binaries.

- FFmpeg essentials build 8.1.2 by GyanD: https://github.com/GyanD/codexffmpeg/releases/tag/8.1.2 . The build is GPLv3; source information is on that release page and at https://ffmpeg.org/ .
- yt-dlp: https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/releases/latest . Its executable includes components under several licenses; see the project's `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt`.

The C# application uses the Vortice.Direct3D11 and Vortice.D3DCompiler NuGet packages during compilation.
